# Prompt gambar gedung penting (Higgsfield → Tripo)

Alur: buat gambar di Higgsfield → pilih yang terbaik → unggah ke Tripo (image-to-3D) → unduh GLB → kirim di chat.

**Gaya bersama (tempel di awal setiap prompt):**
> Stylized 3D game asset, Pixar / Disney animated movie style, cute and colorful, soft rounded shapes, clean painted textures, warm sunny lighting, isometric three-quarter view from the front-left, full building visible, centered, isolated on plain light grey background, no people, no text, no watermark, no ground clutter.

**Tips untuk Tripo:** satu gedung per gambar, sudut 3/4 depan, latar polos, tanpa bayangan panjang. Kalau Tripo minta beberapa sudut, buat juga versi "front view" dan "back view" dengan prompt yang sama.

---

## 1. Gedung tua Barongsai (pintu masuk Lab rahasia)
> …an old abandoned Chinese lion dance (barongsai) temple building on a small hill. The whole front facade is shaped like a giant faded red-and-gold barongsai lion head: big round eyes, golden horn on the forehead, bushy cream fur eyebrows and beard, the wooden door is the lion's open mouth with white teeth. Two-tier curved Chinese temple roof with upturned corners, some missing roof tiles, faded paper lanterns, cracked red pillars, green vines growing over the walls, a few wooden boards nailed across the windows. Mysterious but friendly, not scary.

## 2. Sekolah Glen dan Aara
> …a cheerful Indonesian primary school (sekolah dasar) building, two floors, long white and light-blue walls with many classroom windows, red tiled roof, a covered walkway, a flag pole with the red-and-white Indonesian flag in front, a small school yard, a sign board above the entrance (blank), potted plants along the wall.

## 3. Pasar tradisional
> …a lively Indonesian traditional market hall (pasar tradisional), open-sided wide roof on wooden and green steel pillars, rows of colourful stalls with striped awnings, crates of fruit and vegetables, hanging baskets, a big arched entrance gate, warm and busy feeling.

## 4. Gudang Pasar (tempat Tikus Rakus)
> …an old market warehouse, brick and corrugated-metal walls, big sliding wooden door half open, stacked sacks of rice and wooden crates outside, a small loading platform, slightly messy with crumbs, fun not dirty.

## 5. Rumah Glen
> …a cozy Indonesian family house, one floor with a small upper attic, cream walls with orange-red tiled roof, wooden front porch with two chairs, green window shutters, a small front garden with flowers and a mango tree, a bicycle parked by the fence, a mailbox.

## 6. Ruko (rumah toko) — 2 variasi
> …a two-storey Indonesian shophouse (ruko), shop with folding metal shutter and colourful awning on the ground floor, living floor above with a small balcony and plants, flat roof with a water tank, bright paint (variation A: mint green, variation B: peach orange).

## 7. Rumah warga — 3 variasi
> …a small cute Indonesian village house, one floor, tiled roof, small porch, window shutters, flower pots (variation A: light yellow walls, B: light blue walls, C: pink walls).

## 8. Rumah Sakit
> …a friendly small-town hospital, white building with a big red cross sign, glass entrance doors with a canopy, an ambulance parking spot, neat green hedges, two floors.

## 9. Bank
> …a small classic town bank, cream stone facade with four columns, wide steps, a big gold coin emblem above the door (no text), clean and tidy.

## 10. Menara penanda distrik (5 variasi)
> …a tall decorative town landmark tower, about 12 metres, with a viewing platform on top. Variation A: clock tower (gold). B: bell tower (blue). C: water tower (pink). D: lantern tower with red Chinese lanterns (orange). E: tower topped with a giant gold coin (teal).
